# Copyright & takedown policy

> **Draft for review — not legal advice.** This is the process for rights holders
> to report content and for us to respond. It's written to become a public page
> (e.g. `/copyright`, linked from the footer and Terms). The DMCA
> "designated agent" detail (`[BRACKETS]`) must be filled with a real name and
> address for the safe-harbor process to work.

## Our content and its sources

Travel Roamer publishes content from licensed, attributed sources. We do not host
user-uploaded media. Summary (full detail in [`source-registry.md`](../source-registry.md)):

| Source | What | License | Obligation we meet |
|---|---|---|---|
| NPS / RIDB | Park info, fees, alerts | US public domain | "Data courtesy of NPS" |
| OpenStreetMap | Trail geometry | ODbL | "© OpenStreetMap contributors" on maps |
| Wikimedia Commons | Photos | Per-image CC / PD | Per-image creator + license credit |
| USGS | Elevation | US public domain | Attributed |
| Open-Meteo, MapTiler | Weather, map tiles | Provider terms | Attributed per terms |

If you believe something here infringes your rights despite this, use the process
below and we'll act.

## Reporting infringing content (copyright)

Send a notice to our designated agent:

- **Email:** `[COPYRIGHT CONTACT EMAIL]` (subject: "Copyright notice")
- **Designated agent:** `[AGENT NAME]`, `[MAILING ADDRESS]`

To be actionable (tracks DMCA §512(c)(3)), include:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
3. The URL(s) of the material on Travel Roamer you want removed, specific enough
   for us to locate it.
4. Your contact information (address, phone, email).
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use isn't authorized by the
   owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you
   are the owner or authorized to act for them.

## Our process

1. **Acknowledge** within `[2 business days]`.
2. **Review** the notice. Because our content is licensed and attributed, we may
   first check whether the issue is a fixable attribution error rather than
   infringement.
3. **Act:** where the claim is valid, **remove or disable the material promptly**
   (target `[3 business days]`) and record the action.
4. **Notify** the affected party (if any) so they can respond.

## Counter-notification

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification,
send a counter-notice to the same contact with: your signature; identification of
the removed material and its former location; a statement under penalty of perjury
that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake; and your contact
information and consent to jurisdiction. We may restore the material per applicable
law unless the original complainant pursues a legal order.

## Repeat infringers

We terminate access or remove sources responsible for repeated, valid infringement
claims.

  removal path — no legal process required.
